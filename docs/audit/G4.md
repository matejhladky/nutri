# Audit G4 – `content/topics/12-potraviny.yaml`

No point has `safety: true` and no criteria 3c/7f/7h/8b/8d/10c are used. §1 therefore covered every legal or regulatory
claim plus the one child-health claim (BIO vegetables and nitrates). EUR-Lex blocks automated fetches (HTTP 202
challenge), so EU texts were read in the "as adopted" versions on legislation.gov.uk, together with Commission, EFSA and
Czech official pages.
