# Product vision

**Nutri** is a calm, low-friction study companion for one learner preparing for the Czech professional qualification
*Výživový poradce / výživová poradkyně* (NSK 69-042-N), with about two weeks to go. She attended the lectures and has
the course script (Nutriční Institut, 259 pages). She should never have to plan, make flashcards or decide what to
review: she opens the app, presses one button and studies.

## Principles

- **Minimise friction, not cognitive effort.** Retrieval over rereading; spacing; corrective feedback; interleaving;
  explanation in her own words. No typing where thinking suffices.
- **Teach before testing.** A new point is shown as a short explanation (*Výklad*) and retrieved a few cards later.
- **Breadth is a hard requirement.** The written test needs ≥ 80 % overall *and* ≥ 50 % in each of 18 criteria;
  every one of the 11 competencies must be passed. The planner spreads new material across all criteria.
- **Honest progress.** Counts (seen / recalled / needs attention), no mastery percentages, no pass predictions.
- **Faithful to the course.** Teach what the script says; flag contradictions and questionable claims instead of
  silently "correcting" them.

## The exam (official standard)

| Part | Competencies | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Written test (P) | 1 terminology & nutrients · 2 biochemistry & physiology · 3 diagnostics · 4 food technology & labelling | 75 questions / 75 min; pass ≥ 80 % overall and ≥ 50 % per criterion |
| Oral (Ú) | most of 5–11: energy balance, alternative diets, life stages, sports, hydration, diseases, supplements, labelling, body image, eating disorders, communication | model cases (≥ 10) |
| Practical (PR) | intake card, BMR (method + why), energy need, macros, 1-day plan, red flags for a doctor, difficult clients | simulated clients |

The app trains knowledge, calculations, explanation (oral) and case reasoning. It cannot train handling a bioimpedance
analyser, caliper, meal-planning software or live role-play; those need in-person practice.

## Areas

- **Dnes** — today's soft plan and one *Začít* button; suggests stopping when the daily goal is met, allows continuing.
- **Témata** — concise topic library mapped to the script and criteria; practise a single topic (same progress state).
- **Pokrok** — per competency: not seen / started / recalled / needs attention; weak points; recent activity.

Out of scope for now: exam simulation, settings beyond exam date & daily time, authoring UI, gamification, multi-user.
